# My Personal Trainer

Free web app with two profiles (you and your wife), each with its own plan, food targets and history.

## Put it online (GitHub Pages)
1. New public repo, e.g. `my-personal-trainer`.
2. Upload index.html, manifest.json and the three icon PNGs to the top level of the repo.
3. Settings > Pages > Branch: `main`, folder `/ (root)` > Save.
4. Open https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/my-personal-trainer/ in Safari on each iPhone.
5. Share > Add to Home Screen > Add.

## Updating later
Upload a new index.html over the old one. Your history is untouched: it lives on each phone, not in the repo.

## Backups
Profile tab > Save backup (includes both profiles). Restore backup brings it back on any phone.
